Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Hearth is a household assistant for one family: a shared grocery list, pantry, and to-do list. This page says what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, who you are at home (for example "Mom"), and your mobile number. Your number is how you sign in.
  • Your home's lists: what you and your family add to the app: grocery items, pantry status, purchases, tasks, household preferences ("our usual rice"), and the names, diet notes and allergies you enter for family members.
  • Sign-in records: when codes were sent and when you signed in or out, and whether you used an Android phone or an iPhone, to keep accounts safe and stop abuse.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or payment details. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

How it is used

  • To sign you in, by texting a one-time code to your number. We keep only a scrambled form of the code, never the code itself.
  • To share your home's lists with the people in your home, and nobody else.
  • To suggest things from what your home has confirmed before (for example, your usual quantity of tomatoes).
  • To understand a message the app can't read on its own: the message text is sent to our own AI model, which runs on a computer we control. It is not sent to an outside AI company and is not used to train anyone's model.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Your family: everyone who joined your home with its home code sees the home's lists and the family members' names.
  • Service providers: our SMS provider receives your number and the code in order to deliver the text. Our hosting provider stores the data on our behalf.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in codes: deleted 24 hours after they are used or expire.
  • Sign-in records: deleted after one year.
  • Your account and your home's lists: kept until you delete them.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More → Delete my account. This signs you out everywhere and removes your name and number from your account. Things you added to your home's lists stay with your home for the others, marked as added by a former member. When the last person in a home deletes their account, the home's lists are deleted too.

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address below with your number, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Children

Hearth is meant to be set up by adults. Children may be listed as family members by a parent; we do not knowingly let children under 13 create their own accounts.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in codes and session keys are stored only in scrambled form.
